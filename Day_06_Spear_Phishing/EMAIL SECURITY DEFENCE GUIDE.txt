1. SPF
- Authorizes approved email-sending servers.
- Helps detect unauthorized senders.

2. DKIM
- Uses digital signatures for email authentication.
- Helps verify message integrity.

3. DMARC
- Defines how receivers handle failed authentication.
- Provides reports about authentication results.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
- Configure SPF for authorized senders.
- Enable DKIM signing.
- Start DMARC with monitoring.
- Review DMARC reports.
- Move toward quarantine or reject after
  validating legitimate email sources.

5. CONCLUSION
SPF, DKIM and DMARC help organizations
protect their domains against email spoofing
and improve email authentication.
